# TikTok Sans

Official TikTok Sans variable font, distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.

Source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/tiktoksans
Upstream: https://github.com/tiktok/TikTokSans

The upstream variable TTF was converted to WOFF2 with fontTools. No outlines, axes, or character coverage were changed. All weights, width, optical-size, and slant axes are retained.

The complete license is in `OFL.txt`.
